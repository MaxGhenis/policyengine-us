Figure the 2020 unemployment compensation exclusion (IRC 85(c)) on the head's and spouse's own compensation: leave a tax unit dependent's compensation, which is on the dependent's own return, out of the filer's taxable compensation; exclude up to $10,200 for each spouse on a joint return rather than $10,200 in total; and test modified AGI, which leaves out the compensation, against the $150,000 cutoff without subtracting the compensation a second time. Each spouse's taxable compensation is now their own compensation less their own exclusion, and a dependent's is figured with their own exclusion. A dependent's negative compensation input no longer changes the filer's taxable compensation in any year.
