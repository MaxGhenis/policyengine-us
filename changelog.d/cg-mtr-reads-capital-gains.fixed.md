The capital gains marginal tax rate now adds its extra $1,000 as long-term capital gain, so the 26 U.S.C. 1(h) rates, the net investment income tax and the EITC investment income limit apply to it. It used to raise only person-level capital_gains, which only adjusted gross income read, so the gain was taxed at ordinary rates and the rate missed the net investment income tax and the EITC cliff. This changes capital gains behavioral responses in reforms with a capital gains elasticity. The EITC investment income test and the net investment income tax's capital gain line now read each filer's capital_gains, so a capital_gains amount supplied directly counts there as it does in adjusted gross income.
