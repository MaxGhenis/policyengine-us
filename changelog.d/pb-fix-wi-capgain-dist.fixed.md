Count capital gain distributions reported without Schedule D as long-term capital gain in the Wisconsin, North Dakota, South Carolina, New Mexico and Missouri capital gain subtractions, the Michigan senior interest, dividend and capital gain deduction, the Hawaii and Montana capital gain rate computations, the Montana capital gain credit, and the Arkansas AR1000D. These provisions read only Schedule D gains, so the distributions got no state capital gain relief once they entered federal AGI, and Arkansas, which builds its own gross income, left them out of income entirely.
