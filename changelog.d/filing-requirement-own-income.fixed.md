Apply the federal filing requirement's unearned income test (IRC 6012(a)(1)(C), (f)(2)(D)) only to a head or spouse who can be claimed as a dependent on another return, on that person's own unearned income against the indexed IRC 63(c)(5)(A) amount plus their additional standard deduction (Chart B of the Form 1040 instructions). It applied to every tax unit, against $500, and subtracted every member's earned income, so a dependent's wages could turn the filer's requirement, and the EITC of a unit that would not otherwise file, off. Filers no one can claim are now tested on gross income only (Chart A).
